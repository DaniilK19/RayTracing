# The camera script of the README's GIF (docs/viewer.gif), which scripts/make-gif.ps1 renders with
#   RayTracingViewer --headless --script docs\viewer-tour.txt --frames <folder>
# and turns into a GIF of 12.5 frames a second. Both scenes are triangle meshes, so OptiX renders them. The samples per
# frame are this script's, chosen for the picture: they say nothing of a live window's speed. OptiX's frames and the
# denoiser's depend on the graphics driver.
scene still-life
size 960 540
strata 4
seed 0
hold 10 +1                     # a sample per pixel more each frame: the image itself, noisy
denoise on                     # the window's N
hold 1 +6                      # 16 samples, denoised at once
hold 12 +4                     # refining to 64: denoised again at the doublings, 32 and 64
orbit 16 +8 yaw 20             # a moving view shows the image itself, every frame a new view
hold 1 +8                      # still again: denoised at its first frame, 16 samples
hold 12 +4                     # and at 32 and 64
scene cornell-bust             # Tab, in a window
hold 1 +1                      # denoised at every doubling, from a single sample
hold 1 +1
hold 1 +2
hold 1 +4
hold 1 +8
hold 1 +16
hold 1 +32
hold 1 +64
hold 1 +128                    # 256 samples: the frame the GIF ends on
