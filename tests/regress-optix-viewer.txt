# OptiX in the window, for scripts/verify/hostgate.ps1's chained section regress-optix-viewer (from Step 95;
# chained.txt: kind V, stamped): tiny frames rendered with
#   RayTracingViewer --headless --script tests\regress-optix-viewer.txt --frames <folder> --record <file>
# The graphics driver compiles OptiX's programs, so the kept lines (what the window prints, every frame's SHA-256 and
# the recording's) carry a stamp, the driver's version and the programs' SHA-256: under the stamp of the last accepted
# lines a step's lines must equal them, under another they wait for review (RE-VERIFY).
#
# 40 frames at 96x54 from rounds of 4x4: OptiX switched on and off on the Cornell box (the window's O, here kernel
# lines); --optix on sunlit, then the three scenes of glTF models, named by scene lines in the order Tab takes them
# after sunlit in a session with OptiX (the keys themselves are pressed by tests/window-optix.input, in a window);
# two other strategies on the triangles (M), a camera move and a pass in progress; a model's file, the bust alone in
# the photo studio, and a focus on it through OptiX's pick with the lens open (focus-at at the middle of the image,
# which the bust fills); back to a scene of the books under OptiX, then without it; a mesh scene under kernel auto,
# which traces with OptiX all the same, and a display chosen on its image. The model files are read once each (gpu_scene
# keeps the last two models): the bust and the vase, by still-life.
size 96 54
strata 4
seed 0
# The Cornell box under -p mis (its own strategy, the mixture, isn't OptiX's): the quality kernel, OptiX, then the
# quality kernel again. The frames are saved from f00000, so f00001 and f00004 are both the quality kernel's image at
# 16 passes, before and after OptiX, and must be the same bytes
scene cornell
sampling mis
hold 2 +8
kernel optix
hold 2 +8
kernel auto
hold 1 +16
# --optix on sunlit, then still-life, the scene Tab takes after it in a session with OptiX: its two models read here
kernel optix
sampling scene
scene sunlit
hold 2 +8
scene still-life
hold 2 +8
# M twice on the triangles: nee, then material; then the scene's own (mis) again, and a camera move
sampling nee
hold 2 +8
sampling material
hold 2 +8
sampling scene
orbit 4 +4 yaw 30
# busts (one mesh, three instances) and cornell-bust, there with a pass in progress: 8 passes and the top 20 rows of
# the ninth, then the image completed to 16
scene busts
hold 2 +8
scene cornell-bust
hold 2 +8
frame 8 20 gen 1 size 96 54 lookfrom "278,278,-800" lookat "278,278,0" vfov 40 defocus-angle 0 focus-dist 10
hold 1 +8
# A model's file: the marble bust alone in the photo studio (the cache's, not read again); a lens, and the focus on
# the bust at the image's middle, found by OptiX's pick (the line it prints gives the distance along the ray too)
scene "assets/models/marble_bust_01/marble_bust_01_1k.gltf"
hold 2 +8
camera defocus-angle 3
focus-at 48 27
hold 4 +8
# A scene of the books under OptiX (Tab from a scene that traces with OptiX keeps it, for the books' scenes too), then
# without OptiX (as O turns it off)
scene weekend
hold 2 +8
kernel auto
hold 2 +8
# still-life under kernel auto: its triangle meshes trace with OptiX all the same; then a display of the script's own
# on OptiX's image, neutral at +1 EV, and AgX at 0 EV again (the scene's own)
scene still-life
hold 2 +8
tonemap neutral
exposure 1
hold 2 +8
tonemap agx
exposure 0
hold 3 +8
