# The window's tone maps for scripts/verify/hostgate.ps1 (from Step 75): tiny frames through every display, rendered
# with
#   RayTracingViewer --headless --script tests\regress-tone.txt --frames <folder> --record <file>
# Their bytes, the recording and what the window prints must stay the same from step to step (each step compares with
# the one before: Step 70's window has no tone maps). The Cornell box in the books' bytes, then the same 16 passes
# shown again through agx (a frame of the same passes and view renders nothing), a pass in progress through neutral
# (its top 4 rows hold one sample more), a clipped none, and a scene change under a display chosen by the script,
# which holds for every scene, then back to the books' bytes.
size 96 54
strata 4
seed 0
scene cornell
frame 16 0 gen 1 size 96 54 lookfrom "278,278,-800" lookat "278,278,0" vfov 40 defocus-angle 0 focus-dist 10
tonemap agx
exposure 0.5
frame 16 0 gen 1 size 96 54 lookfrom "278,278,-800" lookat "278,278,0" vfov 40 defocus-angle 0 focus-dist 10
tonemap neutral
exposure -1
frame 16 4 gen 1 size 96 54 lookfrom "278,278,-800" lookat "278,278,0" vfov 40 defocus-angle 0 focus-dist 10
tonemap none
exposure 2
frame 17 0 gen 1 size 96 54 lookfrom "278,278,-800" lookat "278,278,0" vfov 40 defocus-angle 0 focus-dist 10
scene light
hold 1 +16
tonemap books
exposure 0
hold 1 +4
