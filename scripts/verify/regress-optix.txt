# OptiX's renders for scripts/verify/hostgate.ps1's chained section regress-optix (from Step 89; chained.txt: kind G,
# stamped): one RayTracingGPU command's arguments a line, each rendered by hostgate with -o NAME.pfm -o NAME.png into a
# folder of its own. The graphics driver compiles OptiX's programs and brings OptiX's denoiser, so the kept lines (the
# Scene, View, Kernel, Mean radiance, Rays traced, Shadow rays and Denoised lines, and every file's SHA-256, the
# -denoised files' included) carry a stamp, the driver's version and the programs' SHA-256: under the stamp of the last
# accepted lines a step's lines must equal them, under another they wait for review (RE-VERIFY).
#
# Tiny renders (96 by 54 pixels, 16 samples) of the kinds of geometry and light the OptiX tracer handles: quads and a
# lamp (cornell), every kind with media and textures (final), an environment map and GGX metals (studio), moving
# spheres (bouncing), media bounded by quads (smoke), an image texture on a sphere (earth); and the three combinations
# of the quality kernel's integrator, material, nee and mis.
cornell --optix -p mis -w 96 --height 54 -s 16
final --optix -p mis -w 96 --height 54 -s 16
studio --optix -p mis -w 96 --height 54 -s 16
bouncing --optix -p material -w 96 --height 54 -s 16
smoke --optix -p nee -w 96 --height 54 -s 16
earth --optix -p material -w 96 --height 54 -s 16
# OptiX's denoiser (from Step 94) on a CUDA render, through a tone map: the denoised PNG (the display's bytes of the
# denoised image), its PFM and the Denoised line, beside the plain files
cornell -p mis --denoise --tonemap agx -w 96 --height 54 -s 16
